# Static: design

The approved design lives in Wyatt's Google Drive, where he reads it:
`2 Areas/Apps/Wyatt TV/Docs/Wyatt TV Replacement - Plan.md` (approved Nov 15, 2026).

Short version, so this repo stands on its own:

- **Why:** Wyatt TV and Morning Static were deleted because they didn't bring in fresh, good content. Wyatt TV only shuffled a hand-kept channel list.
- **What:** one page he opens instead of YouTube. It already has something worth watching, and a refresh always brings something new.
- **Goals:** a picker that stays fresh (his channels plus channels he has never seen); reroll never repeats; no Shorts, minimum lengths, only his topics; it learns from thumbs; then a live guide, a football Sunday board and a daily newspaper.
- **Not doing:** playing Sunday Ticket games inside the app (they only play on YouTube; the app links out); downloading videos; anything with a monthly fee; access for anyone but Wyatt.
- **Failure looks like:** picks go stale or repeat; he has to keep channel lists by hand; he still ends up on YouTube's home page.
- **Approach picked:** rules plus a handful of YouTube topic searches a day. A Claude "daily editor" is a later add-on for the paper.
- **Rounds:** 1 picker, 2 live guide, 3 football, 4 newspaper. Wyatt asked for all four in one build (Nov 15).
- **Pass test for the picker:** after a week, he opens it before YouTube most days.
